Researcher in Computer Vision

My research interests span Computer Vision, Machine Learning, AI Safety, and AI for Science. I focus on developing safe, accurate, and efficient vision systems and machine-learning models that can reliably perceive, understand, and act on information, while ensuring robustness, protecting privacy, and aligning with societal values.

I received my B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from Universidad Industrial de Santander, Colombia, in 2015, 2018, and 2022, respectively. I was a research intern at the Stanford Vision and Learning Lab (SVL), supervised by Prof. Juan Carlos Niebles. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at KAUST, working with Prof. Bernard Ghanem.

[Note] I am always looking for talented Ph.D. students for internships at KAUST. If you have a research idea that connects with my research interests, I would be happy to hear from you. Feel free to send me an email at carlos DOT hinojosa AT kaust DOT edu DOT sa.
