Research Scientist at KAUST

My research interests span Computer Vision, Machine Learning, AI Safety, and AI for Science. I focus on developing safe, accurate, and efficient vision systems and machine-learning models that can reliably perceive, understand, and act on information, while ensuring robustness, protecting privacy, and aligning with societal values.

I received my B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from Universidad Industrial de Santander, Colombia, in 2015, 2018, and 2022, respectively. I was a research intern at the Stanford Vision and Learning Lab (SVL), supervised by Prof. Juan Carlos Niebles. I am currently a Research Scientist at KAUST in the Image and Video Understanding Lab (IVUL), led by Prof. Bernard Ghanem, where I lead the AI Safety research theme.
